How the program works

Three phases, each one building on the last. You train 3 times a week for 40 minutes, with dumbbells or just your bodyweight.

Weeks 1–2

Foundation

We fix your form on the key movements and find your starting weights. Nothing heavy yet.

Weeks 3–5

Build

Weights go up a little every week. Your plan adjusts to how each session felt.

Weeks 6–8

Peak

Your hardest and most rewarding weeks. On test day you repeat week 1 and see the difference.
