FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Up to 3 people and 3 boards, for as long as you like. No card needed.

Can I import from another tool?

You can import tasks from a CSV file or paste a list, and TaskFlow turns each line into a card.

What happens after the trial?

You choose a plan or drop back to Free. Nothing is deleted and you won't be charged automatically.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in the EU, encrypted at rest and in transit. You can export everything anytime.
