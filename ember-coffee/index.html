Our coffee

From farm to your cup, in three steps

We buy green beans directly from three family farms, roast them ourselves every Tuesday, and only serve coffee that's between 5 and 25 days old.

  1. Sourced directPartner farms in Colombia, Ethiopia and Guatemala, paid well above fair-trade price.
  2. Roasted on siteSmall 5 kg batches in the back room, so every bag has a roast date on it.
  3. Brewed with careEvery barista trains for four weeks before they make your first coffee.
